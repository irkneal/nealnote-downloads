NealNote Studio

Turn a YouTube link into a clean transcript and a polished Word document, saved on your computer and ready to review.

Download for Windows

Install the desktop app, paste a YouTube URL, and generate your document locally.

Download NealNote Studio File: NealNote-Studio-1.3.3-x64.exe

What It Does

  • Accepts a YouTube video URL.
  • Creates transcript and Word document outputs.
  • Saves generated files locally.

Local Output

  • Generated files are saved on your machine.
  • The app keeps the workflow simple and focused.
  • No browser setup is required for the desktop beta.
